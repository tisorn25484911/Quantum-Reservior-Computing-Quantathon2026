Cross-dataset comparison

Runs DataBase_Analysis/compare.py over every dataset: a full Lyapunov and spectral pass, then the comparison columns that put the tiers on a common footing — Lyapunov time in days, samples per Lyapunov time, and Tλ against the dominant period.

This is the existing analysis, not a re-implementation. The numbers and figures are produced by the same compare.collect() / compare.figure() the CLI uses, so this page and python compare.py agree by construction.

A full pass is a Lyapunov fit per dataset. Measured: ~30 s for the chaotic tier, ~3 min for all 17. It runs in the background, so you can leave the page. (The O(N²) neighbour search is already capped at max_n=5000 by lyapunov.py, so the 175k-sample records do not dominate.)
