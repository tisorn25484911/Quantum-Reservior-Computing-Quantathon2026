The conclusion, stated honestly

On the window a farm can act on, the quantum forecast gives the best warning{% if s.best_quantum %} — catching {{ (s.best_quantum.rec3 * 100)|round|int }}% of heatwave days three days out{% endif %}. It predicts the event is coming, not merely continuing.

{% if s.persistence %}

Honest caveat: on balanced F1, naive persistence is a genuine tie ({{ '%.3f'|format(s.persistence.f1_short) }} vs {{ '%.3f'|format(s.best_quantum.f1_short) }}) — because a marine heatwave lasts ≥ 5 days, so “today's heat continues” bets well. The case rests on the aquaculture cost asymmetry (a missed heatwave loses a grow-out cycle; a false alarm just runs an aerator), under which recall is the priority — and the quantum forecast wins there.
