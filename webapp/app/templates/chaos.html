Predictability — how far ahead can this series be forecast?

Before any forecast result means anything, you need the ceiling the dynamics themselves impose. That ceiling is the Lyapunov time Tλ = 1/λ₁: the timescale on which an initial error grows by a factor of e. In steps, H_max = Tλ / dt.

A forecast that keeps skill well past H_max is evidence of leakage, not of a good model. One that dies far short of it is underperforming the physics rather than hitting a wall.
